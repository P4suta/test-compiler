# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing concrete behavior, and keep
technical disagreement focused on evidence. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxing, and deliberate disclosure of secrets or private data are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the project and its participants. Immediate safety concerns may
also be reported through GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Security vulnerabilities
must use the repository's private security-advisory channel instead of a public
issue.
